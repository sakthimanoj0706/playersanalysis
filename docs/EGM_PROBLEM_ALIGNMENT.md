## 1. Official Hackathon Problem Statement & Primary Audit Question

> **Official Challenge:**  
> *"Identify the batters and bowlers who perform most consistently under pressure compared with their own usual performance levels."*

### Primary Audit Question
**Are the selected Top 10 batters and bowlers genuinely the players who perform most consistently under pressure relative to their own usual performance?**

**Direct Evidence-Backed Answer:** **NO — UNCERTAIN / PARTIAL AT BEST (WITH SEVERE STRUCTURAL DISTORTIONS).**
1. **In Bowling (`TOP_10_BOWLERS.csv`):**
   - **8 out of the Top 10 bowlers (`80%`)**—including **#1 Matheesha Pathirana (`0.948x`)**, **#3 Mohammad Rishad Hossain (`0.848x`)**, **#4 Arshdeep Singh (`0.834x`)**, **#5 Ravi Bishnoi (`0.965x`)**, **#6 Taskin Ahmed (`0.833x`)**, **#7 Wanindu Hasaranga (`0.749x`)**, **#8 Avesh Khan (`0.861x`)**, and **#9 Ish Sodhi (`0.875x`)**—have `raw_dot_maintenance < 1.000`, meaning their dot-ball percentage **degrades** under pressure compared with their "normal" baseline.
   - **3 out of the Top 10 bowlers (#4 Arshdeep Singh, #6 Taskin Ahmed, #8 Avesh Khan)** perform **worse under pressure than their normal baseline on BOTH dot-ball percentage AND wicket rate** (`Arshdeep: 0.834x dot, 0.886x wkt`; `Taskin: 0.833x dot, 0.906x wkt`; `Avesh: 0.861x dot, 0.989x wkt`).
   - Why? Because `V3PressureModel.compute_bowling_defensive_pressure` (`src/pressure_engine/v3_pressure_model.py:221-254`) classifies **`52.55%` (`101,935 / 193,988`) of all deliveries as High/Extreme Pressure (`> 6.50`)**, **`36.91%` (`71,592`) as Moderate Pressure (`4.50 < score <= 6.50`)**, and **only `10.55%` (`20,461` total; `19,816` legal) as Normal Pressure (`<= 4.50`)**. In the Powerplay, `0.65%` of legal balls are "Normal" (`0.00%` when 0–2 wickets are down because `c_resource(3.0) + c_phase(2.0) = 5.0 > 4.50`); in the Death overs, `5.06%` are "Normal". Consequently, **"Normal" bowling in V3 is not a bowler's usual performance level—it is an ultra-low-leverage middle-over state after 3–9 wickets have already fallen (`87.75%` Middle overs, `98.94%` with $\ge 3$ wickets down)**!
   - Enforcing `MIN_NORMAL_BALLS_BOWLER = 100` (`scripts/compute_v3_rankings.py:51`) on that `10.55%` slice **disqualifies 145 out of 184 (`78.80%`) crisis-eligible frontline bowlers**, including **120 out of 132 (`90.91%`) frontline fast bowlers** (Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Mohammed Siraj, Kagiso Rabada, Trent Boult, Josh Hazlewood, Jofra Archer, Pat Cummins, Mohammed Shami, Mitchell Starc, Mark Wood, Mark Adair, Obed McCoy, Craig Young). And for **Jasprit Bumrah (`#27`, `1,094` crisis balls, `121` norm balls)**, comparing his `42.32%` Powerplay/Death crisis dot rate against his `59.50%` middle-over tailender normal dot rate produces a `0.711x` maintenance ratio that buries him at Rank #27 behind #10 Gudakesh Motie!
2. **In Batting (`TOP_10_BATTERS.csv`):**
   - `V3PressureModel.compute_batting_crisis_pressure` (`src/pressure_engine/v3_pressure_model.py:149-183`) classifies **`7.97%` (`15,464 / 193,988`) of deliveries as High/Extreme Crisis (`> 5.50`)**, of which **`78.6%` occur in the Death overs** or when 4+ wickets have fallen (`0.24%–0.37%` of balls faced by batting orders 1–3 are crisis).
   - Consequently, world-class top/middle-order pressure batters are structurally excluded by `crisis_balls >= 40`: **Nicholas Pooran** (`39` crisis balls, `210.26` crisis SR vs `157.64` normal SR — excluded by **1 ball**!), **Tristan Stubbs** (`39` crisis balls — excluded by **1 ball**!), **Hardik Pandya** (`38` crisis balls — excluded by **2 balls**!), **Glenn Phillips** (`34` crisis balls, `238.24` crisis SR), **Jos Buttler** (`15` crisis balls), **Heinrich Klaasen** (`15` crisis balls), **Suryakumar Yadav** (`13` crisis balls, `238.46` crisis SR), and **Virat Kohli** (`9` crisis balls).
   - Meanwhile, **#5 Jaker Ali Anik (`139.68` crisis SR)** and **#9 Iftikhar Ahmed (`127.50` crisis SR on `40` balls)**—both of whom strike **below the 39-player eligible cohort mean (`140.20`)**—are elevated into the Top 10 ahead of **Ramandeep Singh (#11, `155.71` SR)**, **Marcus Stoinis (#12, `163.38` SR)**, and **Rinku Singh (#13, `150.82` SR on `122` crisis balls)** because:
     1. Their normal strike rates are at the bottom of the cohort (`120.96` for Iftikhar, #38/39; `122.38` for Jaker Ali, #37/39), inflating `crisis_sr / norm_sr`.
     2. Unweighted match-level CV (`std_sr / mean_sr` across all matches with $\ge 1$ crisis ball, where **47.0% of crisis batter-matches are 1 or 2 balls**) rewards Iftikhar's 1–6 ball singles/twos (`consistency_score = 0.5497`, #1 in cohort $\rightarrow$ `20.00 / 20.00` pts) while penalizing finishers who hit sixes in 1–2 ball cameos (`6(1) = 600 SR` spikes `std_sr`, slashing Rinku Singh to `9.13 / 20` pts and Jitesh Sharma to `4.12 / 20` pts).
