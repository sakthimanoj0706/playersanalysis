## 2. Direct Answers to the 7 Mandatory Audit Questions

### 1. Does the application solve the actual hackathon problem?
**PARTIALLY — NOT RELIABLY IN ITS CURRENT STATE.**  
While the platform has a clean architecture, verified canonical ID resolution, strict extras quarantine, and reproducible execution, **the current Top 10 Batters and Top 10 Bowlers do not reliably represent the players who perform most consistently under pressure compared with their own usual performance levels**:
- **8 of the Top 10 Bowlers perform worse in dot-ball percentage under pressure than in their normal baseline (`raw_dot_maintenance < 1.0`)**, and **3 of the Top 10 (#4 Arshdeep Singh, #6 Taskin Ahmed, #8 Avesh Khan) perform worse under pressure on BOTH dot-ball percentage and wicket rate**.
- **90.91% (120 of 132) of frontline fast bowlers** are excluded from the bowling leaderboard because "Normal Bowling Pressure (`<= 4.50`)" is mathematically impossible (`0.00%`) in the early Powerplay (`0–2` wickets down) and `87.75%` confined to post-collapse middle overs ($\ge 3$ wickets down).
- **2 of the Top 10 Batters (#5 Jaker Ali Anik at `139.68` SR and #9 Iftikhar Ahmed at `127.50` SR)** strike below the eligible cohort average (`140.20`), promoted by low normal baselines (`122.38`, `120.96`) and unweighted match-CV across `1–6` ball cameos that punishes explosive boundary hitters.
- **Mandatory Deliverable 3f ("why each of the 20 players is selected") is incomplete**, as 11 of the 20 players lack individual selection rationales in the methodology report and dashboard.

### 2. Which thresholds are currently unjustified?
1. **`MIN_NORMAL_BALLS_BOWLER = 100` (`norm_balls >= 100`) when paired with `bowling_defensive_score <= 4.50` (`10.66%` of legal balls):** Unjustified because it measures post-collapse middle-over bowling (`87.75%` Middle overs, `98.94%` with $\ge 3$ wickets down) rather than a bowler's usual performance level, purging **145 of 184 (`78.80%`) crisis-eligible bowlers** (`90.91%` of frontline fast bowlers) and subjecting surviving pacers (e.g. Jasprit Bumrah, Rank #27) to severe phase confounding.
2. **Batting `crisis_balls >= 40` when paired with `batting_crisis_score > 5.50` (`7.97%` of balls):** Unjustified because (a) it acts as a lower-order entry filter (`0.24%–0.37%` of balls for batting orders 1–3), excluding Nicholas Pooran (`39b`, `210.26` SR) and Tristan Stubbs (`39b`) by 1 ball and Hardik Pandya (`38b`) by 2 balls; (b) 40 balls yields median 95% CIs of **`100.88` SR points** across the Top 10; and (c) moving to `60` balls eliminates `50%` of the Top 10 (`90%` at `80` balls, `100%` at `150` balls).
3. **Batting `norm_balls >= 30`:** Too low for a stable denominator ($\pm 32.8$ SR standard error at 30 balls).
4. **Role classifier career cumulative ball cutoffs (`bb >= 100 and bf >= 80` for `BOWL_PRIMARY_AR`; `bb >= 40` for `BAT_PRIMARY_AR`):** Unjustified because they are not normalized by matches played or batting order, misclassifying #9–#11 tailender bowlers (Arshdeep, Bishnoi, Taskin, Sodhi, Maharaj, Motie) as All-Rounders and misclassifying 32 bowlers (`bf < 60, m_order <= 7`) as `BAT_SPECIALIST`.

### 3. What threshold ranges are defensible based on available evidence?
- **If Pressure Tiers Are Recalibrated (Recommended):**
  - Define **Bowling Normal Baseline** as all non-crisis deliveries (`bowling_defensive_score <= 6.50`, covering `47.45%` of deliveries) with **phase-matched baseline adjustment** (weighting each bowler's normal dot/wicket/boundary baseline by their crisis Powerplay/Middle/Death phase mix). Then **`min_crisis_balls` in `[120, 150]`**, **`min_normal_balls` in `[120, 200]`**, and **`min_crisis_matches` in `[8, 10]`** retain `120+` frontline pace and spin bowlers on an equal footing with tight CIs (`< 8.5%` median width).
  - Calibrate **Batting Crisis Pressure** to capture the top `~18–20%` of high-leverage deliveries (`batting_crisis_score >= 4.50` or including 2nd-innings chase context). Then **`min_crisis_balls` in `[60, 80]`**, **`min_normal_balls` in `[100, 150]`**, and **`min_crisis_matches` in `[8, 10]`** provide both top/middle-order coverage and narrower CIs.
- **If Current Pressure Tiers (`> 5.50` bat, `<= 4.50` bowl) Are Retained Unchanged:**
  - The dataset **cannot** simultaneously support high sample thresholds (`>= 80` bat crisis balls or `>= 100` bowl normal balls) and representative player coverage. Under unmodified tiers, the only workable batter cutoff is **`35 to 45` crisis balls** (with `min_normal_balls >= 60`, stronger shrinkage $K \ge 60$, and ball-weighted consistency), and this limitation must be prominently disclosed.

### 4. Are the current Top 10 rankings reliable?
**NO.**
- **Top 10 Batters:** Only 6 of the 10 batters (#1 Tilak Varma, #2 Matthew Wade, #3 Daryl Mitchell, #4 Liam Livingstone, #7 David Miller, #8 MS Dhoni) are weight-robust, while #5 Jaker Ali Anik (`139.68` SR) and #9 Iftikhar Ahmed (`127.50` SR) are artifacts of unweighted 1–6 ball match consistency and low normal baselines. Furthermore, 5 of the 10 vanish at `60` crisis balls and 9 of the 10 vanish at `80` crisis balls.
- **Top 10 Bowlers:** 8 of the 10 have `raw_dot_maintenance < 1.0` (worse under pressure than normal), 3 of the 10 degrade on both dot % and wicket rate, 8 of the 10 carry misclassified `BOWL_PRIMARY_AR` labels, #10 Gudakesh Motie swings 16 places across weight sets, and `90.91%` of frontline fast bowlers were excluded from eligibility.

### 5. Which major problems must be corrected?
All **9 Group A items (`A-01` through `A-09`)**:
1. Fix the bowling normal baseline (`A-01`) and phase confounding so frontline fast bowlers (Boult, Hazlewood, Rabada, Siraj, Bhuvneshwar, Archer, Cummins, Shami, Starc) are not purged and Jasprit Bumrah is not penalized for middle-over tailender normal spells.
2. Fix the bowling composite (`A-02`) so bowlers who degrade under pressure (`< 1.0x`) are not rewarded with high maintenance scores and so wicket-rate maintenance and runs/boundaries off bat are included.
3. Fix the unweighted 1–2 ball match-CV consistency formula (`A-03`) and low-baseline ratio inflation so sub-cohort-mean batters (`Iftikhar Ahmed #9`, `Jaker Ali #5`) do not outrank genuine high-SR finishers (`Rinku Singh`, `Marcus Stoinis`, `Ramandeep Singh`).
4. Recalibrate batting crisis threshold / eligibility (`A-04`) and shrinkage ($K \ge 60$) so top/middle-order pressure batters (`Nicholas Pooran 39b`, `Tristan Stubbs 39b`, `Hardik Pandya 38b`) are not arbitrarily locked out.
5. Fix `V3RoleClassifier` (`A-05`) and `generate_v3_roles.py` so #9–#11 tailender bowlers (Arshdeep, Bishnoi, Taskin, Sodhi) are classified as `BOWL_SPECIALIST`, the 32 misclassified bowlers (Fabian Allen, Phehlukwayo, etc.) are restored to bowling eligibility, and Super Overs / bench rows are excluded.
6. Add explicit selection rationales for **all 20 selected Top-10 players** (`A-06`, Deliverable 3f) in `report_generator.py` (`.md` & `.pdf`), `README.md`, `TOP_10_BOWLERS.csv`, and `dashboard/app.py`.
7. Clean up or synchronize the **11 stale V2 files** in root and `data/outputs/` and sort `ALL_BATTER_RANKINGS.csv` / `ALL_BOWLER_RANKINGS.csv` by rank (`A-07`).
8. Re-compute shrinkage and normalization dynamically inside the threshold sensitivity loop and fix the dead 10% bowler sample weight (`A-08`).
9. Include wickets and boundaries on wides/no-balls in `last_12_legal_balls` (`A-09`).

### 6. Which minor problems can remain as documented limitations?
- Exact scoreboard target, dynamic RRR, and bowler total economy rate remain quarantined due to `extras1_runs` being an unconfirmed categorical enum (codes 1–7 without extra run magnitude).
- The Wicket Hazard model (`AUC 0.572`), Joint Ridge model, and LightGBM challenger remain documented as `EXPERIMENTAL_NOT_USED_FOR_RANKING`.
- IPL 2027 auction purse and contract salary figures remain documented as `UNVERIFIED_USER_BRIEF_SCENARIO` assumptions (`data/reference/rr_2027_auction_assumptions.csv`).
- Out-of-time validation across future seasons remains a documented limitation.

### 7. What should be improved after the hackathon?
1. Enrich the raw data pipeline with ball-level numeric extra run counts (`wide_runs`, `noball_runs`, `bye_runs`, `legbye_runs`) from official Cricsheet / ESPNcricinfo scorecards to unlock exact dynamic Required Run Rate (RRR), Win Probability Added (WPA), and true bowler economy under pressure.
2. Replace fixed heuristic pressure weights with an empirically calibrated pre-delivery Win-Probability Leverage index trained on historical T20 innings outcomes.
3. Add interactive individual player dossier cards and match-by-match crisis innings/spell drill-downs in the Streamlit dashboard.
