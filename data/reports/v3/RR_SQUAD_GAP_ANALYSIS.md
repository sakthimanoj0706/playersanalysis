**Execution Date:** November 2026

## 1. Executive Summary

Rajasthan Royals' 3rd-place finish in IPL 2026 established an elite young Indian batting core (Sooryavanshi, Jaiswal, Jurel, Parag) and a world-class frontline spearhead in Jofra Archer (25 wickets).

However, the squad's playoff exit in Qualifier 2 against Gujarat Titans exposed critical tactical structural deficiencies:
1. **Death-Over Pace Concession:** No reliable death partner for Archer; Burger, Deshpande, and Sandeep leaked heavy boundaries in overs 16–20.
2. **Indian Pace Drop-Off:** Over-reliance on Archer meant an expensive or injured spell from him directly compromised the match outcome.
3. **Hetmyer Contract Misallocation:** ₹11.00 Cr was tied up in an overseas finisher who scored just 78 runs in 8 matches. Releasing Hetmyer unlocks the primary purse needed to sign frontline bowling reinforcements.

---

## 2. Priority Role Gaps & Evidence Trail

### Gap 1: Indian Frontline Fast Bowler (Priority 1 — CRITICAL)
- **Tactical Objective:** Lead or share the new ball in the Powerplay (overs 1–6) and execute yorkers in the death overs (overs 17 & 19).
- **Current Squad Coverage:** Tushar Deshpande (₹6.50 Cr, 4 wickets in 7 games) and Sandeep Sharma (₹4.00 Cr, 5 wickets in 6 games).
- **Why Insufficient:** In IPL 2026, RR's domestic pace options conceded boundaries at a high rate and managed only 9 wickets across 13 appearances, forcing Archer to bowl out early.
- **Budget Allocation:** ₹10.00 – ₹13.00 Cr.

### Gap 2: Overseas Death-Over Specialist Bowler (Priority 2 — CRITICAL)
- **Tactical Objective:** Partner Jofra Archer at the death to shut down opposition power-hitters in overs 16–20.
- **Current Squad Coverage:** Nandre Burger (₹3.50 Cr, 14 wickets, but new-ball heavy) and Adam Milne (₹2.40 Cr, 1 match, 0 wickets).
- **Why Insufficient:** Burger is an enforcer suited to new-ball hard length; when tasked with death-over yorkers, opposition batters targeted him. An elite slinger or death specialist is vital.
- **Budget Allocation:** ₹8.00 – ₹11.00 Cr.

### Gap 3: Economical Middle-Over Spinner (Priority 3 — IMPORTANT)
- **Tactical Objective:** Choke the run rate through overs 7–15 and create wicket pressure at the other end for Ravi Bishnoi and Ravindra Jadeja.
- **Current Squad Coverage:** Ravi Bishnoi (₹7.20 Cr, 11 wickets in 9 games) and Ravindra Jadeja (₹14.00 Cr, 10 wickets in 12 games).
- **Why Insufficient:** Bishnoi was benched in 7 of 16 matches due to team balance; RR lacked an economical finger spinner or multi-phase spinner to build continuous dot-ball pressure.
- **Budget Allocation:** ₹3.00 – ₹5.00 Cr.

### Gap 4: Domestic Lower-Order Finisher (Priority 4 — IMPORTANT)
- **Tactical Objective:** Provide boundary-hitting depth at No. 6/7, allowing RR to field an overseas bowling specialist in the playing XI.
- **Current Squad Coverage:** Shubham Dubey (₹80 L, 81 runs in 7 games) and Donovan Ferreira (₹1.00 Cr, overseas).
- **Why Insufficient:** Releasing Shimron Hetmyer frees an overseas slot, but RR needs an Indian domestic power hitter to ensure batting depth down to No. 7 without consuming an overseas position.
- **Budget Allocation:** ₹2.00 – ₹3.00 Cr.
