## 1. Candidate Evaluation Matrix Overview

In accordance with Phase 14 of the V3 specification, player fit is evaluated **strictly against the verified role deficiencies** identified in the Rajasthan Royals 2026 season analysis.

Candidates are evaluated without arbitrary composite numerical scores, relying instead on multi-dimensional criteria:
- Tactical fit for the specific RR gap
- Innings phase execution (Powerplay, Middle, Death)
- Batting order compatibility
- Universal performance ranking under pressure
- Complementarity with current RR personnel (Archer, Jadeja, Bishnoi, Sooryavanshi)
- Overseas slot constraint impact (maximum 4 overseas in playing XI)
- Availability and injury risk history
- Projected auction acquisition cost vs. available purse

## 2. Priority 1 Gap: Indian Frontline Fast Bowler

### Candidate 1: Prasidh Krishna (Recommendation: **Strong Fit**)
- **Tactical Match:** Direct upgrade over Tushar Deshpande and Sandeep Sharma.
- **Evidence:** 69 IPL wickets, 40.2% dot balls, elite hit-the-deck pace (145+ kph). Former Rajasthan Royals spearhead with intimate familiarity with Jaipur’s pitch dynamics.
- **Comparison with Current RR Cover:** Deshpande took 4 wickets in 7 games at high boundary concession. Prasidh provides genuine pace, new-ball bounce, and back-of-a-length control.
- **Overseas Impact:** Zero overseas slot impact (Indian domestic slot).
- **Injury Risk:** Medium-High (history of lumbar stress fractures).
- **Target Valuation:** ₹8.00 – ₹11.00 Cr.

### Candidate 2: Avesh Khan (Recommendation: **Strong Fit**)
- **Tactical Match:** Proven death-over defensive specialist capable of executing yorkers under high pressure.
- **Evidence:** 65 IPL wickets; top-tier yorker percentage in overs 18–20.
- **Comparison with Current RR Cover:** Sandeep Sharma struggled with death execution (conceding heavy boundaries); Avesh provides hit-the-deck pace and proven death execution.
- **Overseas Impact:** None (Indian slot).
- **Injury Risk:** Low.
- **Target Valuation:** ₹7.00 – ₹9.50 Cr.

### Candidate 3: Mukesh Kumar (Recommendation: **Conditional Fit**)
- **Tactical Match:** Disciplined wide-yorker execution at the death.
- **Evidence:** India T20I international cap; dependable yorker control.
- **Comparison:** Lacks the outright velocity of Prasidh, but provides tactical control.
- **Target Valuation:** ₹5.50 – ₹8.00 Cr.

### Candidate 4: Akash Deep (Recommendation: **Conditional Fit**)
